ABBOTT WORLD MARATHON MAJORS

From six stars to eight ★★★★★★★☆

The Abbott World Marathon Majors began in 2006 with five races and grew to six when Tokyo joined in 2013. Sydney became the seventh Major in 2025, and Cape Town is confirmed as the eighth from 2027. Today there are seven Majors, listed below in race-calendar order.

Series history

  1. 2006

    Five founding races

    Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago and New York form the World Marathon Majors.

  2. 2013

    Tokyo joins

    The sixth Major and the first outside Europe and North America. Runners who complete all six can now earn Six Star Finisher status; the Six Star Medal followed in 2016.

  3. 2025

    Sydney joins

    The seventh race and the first Major in the Southern Hemisphere.

  4. 2027

    Cape Town joins

    Confirmed in June 2026 as the eighth race and Africa’s first Major.

  5. 2026

    Shanghai under review

    Its 6 December 2026 race is the second assessment. If it passes, it could become the ninth Major.

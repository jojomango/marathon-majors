Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

What this site collects

This is a static website with no accounts, comments or forms. It never asks for your name, email address or other personal details.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4, which sets analytics cookies, to count:

  • which race pages you view, for how long, and in which interface language
  • your approximate region and the type of device and browser you use
  • which website linked you here

The data is only used to understand which content is popular and to improve the site. IP addresses are anonymised. Google processes and stores the data under its privacy policy.

How this works depends on where you are. The site tells this from your browser’s time zone and does not look up your IP address to do so:

  • Europe (including the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland): on your first visit you are asked for consent, and Google Analytics is only loaded after you choose “Accept”. If you choose “Decline”, no tracking code is loaded.
  • Elsewhere: Google Analytics is loaded by default and a notice is shown at the bottom of the page. If you choose “Don’t track me”, the site stops sending data, deletes the analytics cookies and does not load Google Analytics again.

Wherever you are, Google Analytics is never loaded if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.

Language setting

The language you pick in the language switcher is stored in your own browser (localStorage) and is only used to show the same language next time.

Changing your choice

Your analytics cookie choice is also stored only in your browser. Use the button below to clear it, and you will be asked or notified again the next time you open the site. You can also clear the site data in your browser.

Third-party resources

Web fonts are provided by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers to load them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep access logs under its own policies.

Contact

For questions about this notice, please open an issue on the site’s GitHub repository.

This is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with any race organiser or with Abbott World Marathon Majors. Check each race’s official website for the actual course, dates and rules.

Routes: rebuilt from each race’s published official course map using OpenStreetMap road data (distances scaled to 42.195 km). Cape Town’s 2027 course is not published yet, so its route is still approximate. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL.

Elevation: USGS 3DEP for the three US races; Copernicus EU-DEM for London and Berlin; Geospatial Information Authority of Japan for Tokyo; NASA SRTM for Sydney; retrieved through OpenTopoData and smoothed. Bridge decks are not captured. Every profile uses the same vertical scale (160 m), so races can be compared directly.
